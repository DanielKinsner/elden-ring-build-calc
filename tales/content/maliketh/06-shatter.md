## VI. Shatter

She broke it.

I was not there. That too. I was already in the east, or on the road to the east, following the cold in the gap where the piece had been; I had left her house because I could not hold the door of it any more, being the door of something else. So I did not see her lift the hammer. I have seen the hammer since, in the hands of men who found it; it is a stone thing from her country outside, and it is cracked, because the Ring cracked it as it went.

I felt it.

Every seal feels the thing it seals a door for. There was a moment, on the road, with the red country coming up ahead of me like a wound, when the whole weight I was carrying inside me suddenly had nothing to be weight *for*. The Ring broke. I felt it break the way I had felt the half-wheel break, but larger, all at once, every rune at once, the gold going into pieces on the floor of the world. And the tree closed over her like a fist.

I stopped walking. I remember that. I stood in the road with my hands open at my sides, the way her hands had been closed, and the ground under me, which had leaned away from me since the night I put the point to my breast, stopped leaning. It did not come back to me. It only stopped knowing what I was. The thing I carried had been the one dark rune of a ring of gold, and the ring was gone, and what was I the dark of now? I stood there until it was night and then I went on, because a following goes on; that is all it knows how to do; and there was nothing in front of me, and I went on toward nothing, east.

Why shatter.

I have that question too. Two questions, then, for her: why gull me, and why shatter. She has had the whole of time to answer them in, and she has used none of it.

Understand what she broke. Not me. I am not so proud; a shadow is not a thing that can be broken; it only goes where the light does not. She wished me to be the seal of Death within the Order. That was what she wished. And then she broke the Order.

A seal on a broken door. A lock on a room with no walls. I was still sealing. I am still sealing; I will be sealing when you come through that door. There was nothing left to seal *for*. That is the betrayal, if I am to use the word the ones who write about me use. Not that she betrayed me. I do not know that she did. I know that she betrayed the thing she wished me to be. She made a place for Death and put me in it and then she broke the house the place was in, and left me standing in the one room that was still whole, holding the door shut against a hall that was not there.

Her children fought over the pieces.

You know this. Everyone knows this; it is the age you were born into, if you were born. They took up the shards of the gold and warred, and none of them won, and the ones who did not die became things that could not, and the land went red and rotten and mad with their warring. I did not go to them. They were her children. I was her shadow. A shadow does not go to war for the pieces of a thing. It follows. And there was no longer anything in front of me to follow. There was only the cold in the gap, calling from the east.

*More.*

*I am not sated.*

*Feed me more. Death.*
