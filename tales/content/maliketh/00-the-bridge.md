## The Bridge

*being the testament of Maliketh, the Black Blade, who was also Gurranq*

*Maliketh was a shadowbound beast given to his Empyrean. Marika's sole need of her shadow was a vessel to lock away Destined Death. Even then, she betrayed him.*

Remembrance of the Black Blade, hewn into the Erdtree

You are on the bridge.

I know the bridge by the sound it makes when something living crosses it. Stone does not care what walks on it, but it tells me. Everything in this city tells me, because I am the only thing left in it that is still listening. The dragons do not listen; they wheel in the storm and scream at the lightning that is their own hide. The lord of the storm, behind his door at the heart of the city, the dragon with more heads than a beast can count, does not listen. He waits, which is a different thing. I have learned the difference in the time I have had here, and time here does not run. It pools. A thing that has stood in it long enough can be in two places in it at once. You will hear what I mean before I explain it.

Thou, who approacheth Destined Death.

That is what I will say when you come through the door, because it is what I have said to every one of you, and because it is true, and because it is the last true thing before the fighting starts. But you are not through the door. You are on the bridge, and the bridge is long, and the storm makes it longer. So I will tell you what you are climbing toward.

Not to turn you back. Nothing has ever turned back. I have stood in this hall and said my sentence to the ones who came before you, and they came on, and they are in the hall still, in pieces, under the dust that does not settle. I will tell you so that when it is over, and you stand above me, and I ask you the one question I have left, you will not be able to say you did not know what it was you killed.

I have had three names. I will give them to you in the order I had them, which is, near enough, the order I lost them.
