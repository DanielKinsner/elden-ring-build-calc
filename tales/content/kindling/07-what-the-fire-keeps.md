## VII. What the Fire Keeps

In most turnings of the story, that is where my journey ends.

You release Destined Death. The thorns burn away. You enter the Erdtree and decide what kind of age will follow.

What remains of me is only warmth in the roots, perhaps, and a name you remember when you rest beside grace.

But fire knows by contact. It feels what feeds it, what resists it, and what the heat changes next. Through that long chain, I felt Death return to the Lands Between.

And with it came older images.

Black flame before its deepest power was sealed. Pale cloth wrapped around newborn apostles. A queenly shape with an eye the color of the first hour of night. The descending shadow of a beast's blade.

The images did not tell me whether that queen died when Maliketh defeated her. They did not call her by my name.

Still, the dusk-colored eye turned toward mine.

Was she my predecessor? My inheritance? Was some remainder of her sealed behind my left eye? Was I her second life? Or did Destined Death, once free, simply show me the shape of what I might become?

I do not know.

It would be easy to say I was always the ancient Gloam-Eyed Queen. Easy answers are hungry things. This one consumes an age of silence to explain a single eye.

Perhaps Death merely found in me a willing hand.

Perhaps inheritance can resemble memory when the inheritance is old enough.

I will not turn resemblance into a crown and call the coronation proof.

Ash keeps heat, not conclusions.

In most worlds, I let the vision go. You walk toward the throne, and my story rests.

But there is another turning.

And if you want to understand me, you must see that one too.
