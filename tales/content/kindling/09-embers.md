## IX. Embers

That is my story, in both its turnings.

I began as a burned spirit with a forgotten purpose. I borrowed the office of a Finger Maiden because I needed your help. Torrent chose you, and I agreed with him.

On the road, the bargain became companionship. I learned the weight of your hand, the persistence of your pulse, and the value of lives too small for rulers to notice.

I found the purpose my mother had given me.

Then I questioned it.

I left when I became free. I returned when freedom allowed me to choose you. I refused a rescue that would have destroyed the world. At the Forge, I accepted an ending I had weighed for myself.

I still do not know everything I was.

Daughter of a Queen, almost certainly. Younger sister to a red-haired sorrow in a shadowed land, perhaps. Heir, vessel, successor, or second coming of a dusk-eyed queen—perhaps.

The Lands Between is full of stories that survive as wounds in other people's stories.

The witch beneath the cold moon has one.

The exiled son beneath the shadowed tree has one.

The Omen who guarded a door that hated him had one.

Perhaps, if you return, they will tell you.

But this one was mine.

I was made for a purpose. Most of us are—by mothers, by orders, by the shape of the age we enter. That is not the tragedy.

The tragedy is to stop there. To live and die as someone else's unread letter.

I read mine.

I weighed it against everything I had witnessed: every rotted throne, every unburied army, every tailor weeping for his mother's voice, every stubborn pulse beneath my borrowed hand.

Then I signed it myself, in fire, across the sky.

I was made for a purpose.

I became a person when I chose what that purpose meant.

If you remember me only as the woman who burned the Erdtree, remember this as well.

The fire was not where my story began.

The fire was where the choice became visible.

And if, on some dark road, a smoldering butterfly crosses your path—small, burning, refusing to be consumed—do an old spirit a kindness.

Do not put it out.

Let it choose.
