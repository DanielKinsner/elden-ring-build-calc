## I. The Fire, and the Beginning

If you remember me at all, you probably remember the fire.

You remember the Forge of the Giants. The flame in my hand. The Erdtree burning against the night.

But that is where my story ended. It is not where our story began.

My name is Melina. When you met me, I was burned and bodiless, carrying a purpose I could no longer remember.

Our story began beneath Limgrave, in a graveyard at the bottom of the world. You had been called back to the Lands Between by grace, crossed the fog, and almost immediately been killed by a creature made from other people's arms.

It was not an impressive beginning.

By then, the Lands Between had been broken for a very long time. Queen Marika had shattered the Elden Ring. Her children had taken its fragments and gone to war over what remained. None of them had won. The kingdom simply stayed wounded, waiting for someone stubborn enough to enter the ruin and decide what should come after it.

Grace began calling the Tarnished home for that purpose. One after another, they returned. Warriors. Prophets. Murderers. Fools. Most followed the golden light for a little while, then lost sight of it—or lost themselves.

Torrent and I watched them come back.

Torrent is the horned spirit-steed who carried you across the Lands Between. People speak of him as if he were merely a horse. He is not. He is older, stranger, and far more selective. He had watched many Tarnished rise from death before you. He had never chosen one.

Then he found your body.

I remember leaning over you. I remember Torrent's breath, and the way his ears stood—not wary, not uncertain. Certain.

“So it is to be this one,” I told him. “Very well.”

You did not hear me. You were still busy being dead.

I left you there to rise again, then waited beside a site of grace in the open fields of Limgrave. When you finally rested, I stepped out of the golden light and offered you an accord.

I told you that you were maidenless. It was not difficult to notice.

A Tarnished needs a Finger Maiden: someone who can turn runes into strength and guide them toward the Erdtree. You had no maiden. I had no living body, and I could not make the journey to the Erdtree alone.

So I offered to play the role.

Carry me to the foot of the Erdtree, I said, and I will turn your runes into strength.

It sounded like a blessing. In truth, it was a bargain between two stranded people, each holding the thing the other lacked.

I also gave you a small golden whistle. When you used it, Torrent came to you.

I told you he had chosen you. That was the first completely honest thing I gave you.

You accepted my accord, and our journey began: you in the saddle, me moving between sites of grace like smoke through firelight. At first, I thought I needed only your legs. You needed only my hands. We would reach the Erdtree, recover the purpose my mother had given me there, and part as neatly as two travelers leaving the same road.

That is what I believed.

I did not yet understand Torrent's choice.

And I did not yet understand you.
