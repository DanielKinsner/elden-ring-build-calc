## XV. The Doll That Spoke

There is a stretch of my history during which the sovereign architect of the age's great treason, demigod, Empyrean-that-was, mistress of the coming night, traveled the underworld in a stranger's pack, wedged — thou hast confessed it since, under questioning — between a coil of rope and a cheese.

We will pass over the cheese. We will not pass over the rest, because this movement, which the ballads would cut for dignity's sake, is where thou and I actually happened. The wedding was only ever the paperwork.

Why a coffin, thou didst never ask — another of thy tactful silences; this movement retires several. When my slumber took me after the blade's delivery, my wolf was southward, my smith was burdened, and my tower — thou hadst proven it thyself with an accusation in thy mouth — my tower could be climbed. A sleeping treason wants moving. There are old roads down Ainsel, and old rites of passage on its water, and a shape my size travels safest as what the deep already understands: a small dead thing, decently boxed, going downriver. Who placed me in that coffin and gave me to the current I cannot name from inside the sleep; no witness left me an account. I know only that the river, which has carried stranger cargo than a witch, kept me. I woke — partially, between tides, the way my sleep half-wakes — in the dark of the box, in the sound of water under the world, and I was, hear the confession plainly, *afraid*. Not of harm. Of the size of my own design, felt for the first time from inside a coffin it had required. Fear, doubt, loneliness: I made a little private inventory of them there in the box years before I would say those three words to the whole world from a higher place. I knew them by their first names already. Remember that, at the ending. Every road I have ever offered anyone, I had walked first in the dark, in a box, alone.

Then the box opened, and the face above it was thine.

Thou hadst come down Ainsel on thy own thread of the design — and there, where the river widens under Nokstella's far lamps, thou foundest a coffin, and in the coffin a doll thou knewest, and thou — I have made thee reenact this; I wanted the choreography exact — thou didst not pry, nor prod, nor pocket me for selling. Thou didst *speak*. To a doll. Repeatedly. At every rest thy road offered, thou tookest me out, set me where a person sits, and tried again, patient as water, courteous as thou art to everything that cannot pay thee back —

and I lay in my half-sleep listening, mortified and measuring, while the qualification I had requisitioned in an armory's grammar talked to a shelf-child's body as though somebody were home.

Somebody was. On the — I have never told thee the count; it was the fourth try — on the fourth try, vanity lost to loneliness, and the doll answered.

*Oh? A dogged fellow, aren't we? Or is it merely thy habit, to talk to dolls?* — arch to the last, even surfacing from sleep in a rucksack; I have no defense and file none. And then, because thy patience had sprung what my design had never budgeted — a conversation — the arch gave way, and what came out was nearer the truth than I had been aloud in a century: *Fine...fine. I hadn't expected any soul to recognise me in this guise. But now the cat is out the bag, I cannot allow thee thy freedoms.*

The cat is out the bag. A century of ciphers and mirrored helms, and the great secret ends in a nursery idiom, spoken from luggage. Keep that sentence. It is the truest portrait of me in the record: a woman who speaks like a treaty until someone is kind to her without a reason, and then speaks like a girl caught out, and hates it, and does not entirely hate it.

Thou carriedst me up through Nokstella itself, and I will set down what that carrying was, for no other soul will ever have the vantage to tell it: the last princess of the moon's own house, entering the city of the elder moon for the first and only time, at boot-height, in luggage. The irony was not lost on me. Very little is. But hear what I saw from the pack's gap, up the long stairs of that drowned marvel — the ziggurats stepped into the dark like patience made architecture; the water-channels still running their courses after an age without a groundskeeper; the silver tears drifting the halls like moments of the past that had declined to disperse; and over it all the false lamps, their courses wheeling. The city of the people who await me, and I crossed it hidden, small, and silent, and could not so much as pay my respects.

I made them privately. Every stair of that climb, I made them. If any Nox soul ever reads this testament under my true stars: thy Lady of the Night has been in thy capital exactly once, love — in a rucksack, between a rope and a cheese, weeping without one tear to do it with, for the beauty of what was kept alive in the dark. Sovereigns arrive in many styles. Mine has range.

What I asked of thee then, I asked in payment's shape because payment was the only shape I could still pronounce: *Perform for me a service, as recompense. Eliminate the Baleful Shadows which prowl these lands.*

Now the dark heart of this movement, and I will take it slowly, because I have never once said it whole.

I told thee, in the doll's small voice, what the Baleful Shadows are: assassins. *Their* assassins — and thou knewest by then who *they* were in my mouth; there was only ever one they. The powers I had robbed and defied do not send armies after an Empyrean gone feral. Armies are for enemies. For property that absconds, they send what one sends after strayed property: the *instrument of recall*. The shadow-craft itself — the same grammar that sewed my wolf to my childhood — turned to its final purpose and set hunting.

Perhaps it was naming their craft aloud that loosened what came next. Perhaps it was only thy silence, making no demand upon the answer. In that small body, before the assassin found us, I told thee what I had never managed to tell either of the men themselves.

*Even when I turned my back upon the Two Fingers, Blaidd remained my loyal ally.* He had been created their vassal for an Empyrean, and by remaining mine he had become, I said, their colossal failure. Then Iji entered the sentence beside him, where he belongs: *Blaidd, and Iji both... Art willing to give too much to me.*

There. It was in me even then: not merely the knowledge that they served, but the knowledge that their giving exceeded my deserving. I let thee hear it once, heard myself hearing it, and recoiled behind the oldest wall I owned. *Ach, this form hath loosened my tongue. I've let slip too much. Forget what thou'st heard. Forget.*

Forget, twice, because by the second saying I had ceased to issue an order and begun to ask for a mercy. Thou gavest me neither obedience nor humiliation. Thou simply kept the words without spending them.

And the one that came for me in Nokstella's dark wore Blaidd's shape.

Thou foundest it in the city of the black moon, between me and my road, wearing the armor I knew, the sword I had watched sworn, the gait, the grin's ghost — the likeness, as a certain mask's chronicler once wrote of another likeness, *striking*. And thou didst cut it down, because I asked thee, and it died — I am told; I was in thy pack; even this I know only by thy report — it died silent, which my Blaidd never did anything.

Hear me say the thing I can prove, and then hear me refuse the thing I cannot. What thou killedst was an assassin of the Two Fingers wearing a wolf's shape. Whether it was *made from* him — cast from my shadow's shadow, printed off his bond the way the silver tears print off the living — or whether it *was* him in any grain that matters, some conscripted sliver of my brother-wolf hollowed and aimed at his own lady —

I do not know. The craft is theirs, not mine; its recipes were never shown me. And because I do not know, I have chosen — hear the choosing; it is the one mercy I have ever granted myself against the evidence — I have chosen to keep the accounts separate. The thing in Nokstella died silent and is nothing's brother. My Blaidd's own ending comes three movements hence, in daylight, with words, and it is his, and I will not let the Fingers' forgery share a grave-ledger with him. If that is bookkeeping as mercy, then just once, the bookkeeper kept a book for her own heart's sake. Audit it if thou darest. I do not.

When it was done, and the way lay clear, I had no payment-shaped phrase left that could hold what thou hadst done. *Beautifully fought,* I told thee. *My thanks. 'Twas more of a challenge than I envisioned. Now I can finally stand before them.* Every word exact, every word still armored. Then the armor failed in a place so small no enemy would have noticed it.

*This is farewell, my dear. Tell Blaidd, and Iji... I love them.*

I love them. Three words. No argument, no account, no moon between the feeling and its name. I had ordered thee to forget the evidence of my tenderness, then made thee its messenger. Thou didst carry the sentence faithfully. Whether it reached either of them before the end is another cruelty I cannot make answer differently by polishing it.

With that farewell I released the last of my mother's keys to thee — the palace key I had discarded with the flesh, the one that opens a chest passed down the princesses of Caria, kept in the Grand Library beside the woman rocking an amber egg. I sent thee to my mother's own room, bearing my death's key, to take up the ring of my house's oldest tradition.

The doll went back in the pack. Between the rope and, yes, the cheese.

I have ridden to war at the head of knights, and I have crossed the sky of this world as its new law. I am not sure I have ever traveled better accompanied.
