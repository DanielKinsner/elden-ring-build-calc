## XI. A Rare Sort

Between the church and the tower, a dead man's work lies on the road, and I will not step over it as though it were not there. Thou hadst, by the time thou climbedst to me, an accusation in thy keeping. I will honor its maker before I tell thee what I did with it.

Rogier. A sorcerer-knight out of no great house, with a rapier's manners and a scholar's disease — the inability to leave a wound unexamined. He had gone down under Stormveil, where a thing lies in the roots that the Order does not advertise, and he had recognized it: a relic of the black knives' plot. Where a wiser man would have resealed the door and prospered, he began to *read*. He read the misshapen dead. He read a knifeprint — the mark my rite left in steel, which I had believed illegible; hear me admit in open court that the man out-read my craft — and in the print he found what he told thee plainly, with his failing body propped in a chapel like a lesson: that the rite upon those blades had an author. That the author orchestrated the Night. And that her name — *her's was the name I discovered in the imprint*, so he put it, and I quote my accuser exactly, for accuracy was his whole estate and I will not rob the dead —

Lunar Princess Ranni.

He asked thee to find me. He asked thee to seek the cursemark on my discarded flesh, that all might be laid bare. He was courteous about it, I am told. He was courteous about everything; it is the armor of men who suspect the world is worse than they can afford to know.

And he was dying of the asking — set that beside the courtesy, for it is what turns his chapter from cleverness to valor. The thing beneath Stormveil that he had gone to read was no inert relic. What sleeps in the roots of that place belongs to the family of powers my Night set loose, and it had touched him for his curiosity — a wound of the roots, past leechcraft, climbing him slowly the whole while he sat propped in his chapel annotating my crimes with a steady hand. He knew what was taking him. He kept working. A scholar with a deathbed's worth of hours chose to spend them completing the indictment of a demigod he had no power to punish and no reward for naming — because the record was crooked, love, and he could not abide a crooked record, and there was no one else. Thou seest why I have called this testament's whole method by his discipline more than once. I never met the man. I am not certain I would have dared. Of all the age's judges, the only one I feared was the one who wanted nothing from the verdict but its accuracy.

He was also, thou wilt have noticed, *right*. On every count a mortal scholar could reach, right. I have unmade better-defended truths than his. I let his stand. Mark it, love: the one contemporary who caught me, I have never once maligned. A sovereign corrects records. She does not burn the accurate ones. There are few enough of them.

So thou camest up my stair with his verdict in thy mouth.

I knew before thou reachedst the top. My tower tells me who climbs it; treason's furniture is mostly wards. And I will give thee now the interior of a moment thou hast only ever seen the outside of. Consider what stood at my window that day: the age's most wanted architect, awake — barely; the tide of sleep was heavy that season — receiving, in her own sanctum, a visitor bearing the first true accusation in a hundred years, from the one investigator who had ever read the rite rightly. My counselors, had I woken them, would have counseled what counselors counsel. The visitor knows. The visitor is nothing: a graveyard Tarnished, deedless, maidenless, twice-dead that fortnight. The tower is tall. The lake keeps secrets.

I did not wake them. I watched thee come up the last turning — dust, patience, that grave-cold economy of motion thou hast never lost — and I made instead the decision this whole movement turns on, and I made it, thou shouldst know, before thou hadst said one word. I decided to *own it*.

Thou didst not accuse. That was thy first astonishment: thou laidst Rogier's words down before me the way one lays a found object on a table — a thing that plainly belonged to me, returned to its owner — and thou waitedst. No sword drawn behind it. Most visitors manage one or the other, accusation or blade. Thou broughtest me evidence and *silence*, which is the interrogation of the very finest order, and I have used it on kings.

So I told thee. Thou rememberest what and how much: I am the witch Ranni. I stole Death long ago, and search now for the dark path. The theft of the rune's fragment; the rite that forged the godslaying knives; the refusal of the Fingers; the flesh slain and cast away. My whole catechism of owned deeds, first-person, unprompted, entire — everything this testament has told thee at length, I gave thee that day in the short form, standing, to a stranger, on the strength of a nod at a midnight fire.

Thou hast wondered since — thou must have — why. Why hand a stranger the confession the age was starving for? Coldly, then, since cold is my honesty: because a secret known is a leash held, and I had sworn to hold no more leashes and wear none. Rogier knew; thou knewest; the only sovereignty left me was to be the *author* of the knowing. Confession, my dear one, is how the cornered stay sovereign. I commend the maneuver to thee. I have never once regretted it.

And warmly — for thou hast taught me the word bears use — because of what thou didst at the catechism's end. Thou didst not flinch, nor bargain, nor reach for the door. Thou didst ask what I *sought*. Not what I had done — I had told thee — but where the dark path led, as though the direction might matter more than the record.

A rare sort, I thought. The exact words. I have kept the thought as I keep everything.

What I sought. Thou hadst earned the answer, and it was this movement's second gift: service.

I offered thee a place in my small treason, and thou tookest it, and I named thee my household so thou shouldst know the size of what thou hadst joined: a half-wolven warrior by the name of Blaidd, whom thou knewest already by his bellow; War Counselor Iji, at the road's forge, who would sell thee honest steel and take thy measure behind his mirrors; and Preceptor Seluvis, of whom I said what courtesy required and not one syllable more. Three retainers, one doll, and now one Tarnished. The armies of the coming age of stars, mustered entire in a juniper garden.

And I set thee thy first labor, which was also the last great piece of my design: the hidden treasure of Nokron, the Eternal City. I did not dress it small. I told thee the city lay under Limgrave, sealed since its sky fell; that the way in was no way at all until the heavens changed; and that what slept in its vaults was the one edge in creation fit for the throat of my last enemy — of whom, patience; her chapter is under the cathedral. Thou didst not ask how a sealed city was to be entered. Thou didst go to find out. I have commanded legions in my time, love. I had forgotten what it is to be simply *believed*.

One more beat belongs to this movement, because the eighteenth will need it on the record.

When thou wast gone down the stair with thy commission, I stood a long while at the window, and the thought I stood with was not of Nokron. It was this: *he brought me the accusation, and stayed to take my orders.* And close behind it, in my crone's voice, the old corollary I had priced everything by: what does that cost, and who pays?

For I had just done a thing I had sworn off with leashes and title-deeds. I had let someone in. Rogier's verdict I could own; it priced only me. But service — service binds two ways, as deeds do. Every soul in my keeping was a hostage the heavens might yet think to take: a wolf with seams I dared not test, a smith whose courage was filed under furniture, and now a stranger who believed me on a nod. I told myself what commanders tell themselves: that the design was near its end, that the cost-curve of loyalty had been paid already, that this last recruit was a borrowed blade, not a belonging.

I record the telling. I record also that I stood at that window until the lake went dark.

I told thee what I had done, and thou stayedst. I have never decided, in all the years since, which of us that indicts. It is the only ledger-line in this testament I have left unsummed on purpose. Some accounts, my dear one, a woman keeps open because closing them would mean knowing the answer.
